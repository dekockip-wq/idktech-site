Your farm watched day and night, even when the internet is down.Jou plaas dag en nag bewaak, selfs as die internet af is.

FarmDiesel

Every litre counted. Every missing litre on your phone.Elke liter getel. Elke liter wat wegraak, op jou foon.

A smart meter on your tank and the logbook in your pocket. You see every fill counted live; diesel that runs with no fill opened, or after hours, reaches you within seconds; and a pump lock keeps the tap shut until someone starts a fill. Your accountant gets the SARS logbooks and VAT201 litres, every figure traced back to its invoice and photo.

'n Slim meter op jou tenk en die logboek in jou sak. Jy sien elke vulling lewendig getel; diesel wat loop sonder dat iemand 'n vulling oopgemaak het, of ná ure, is binne sekondes by jou; en 'n pompslot hou die kraan toe totdat iemand 'n vulling begin. Jou rekenmeester kry die SARS-logboeke en BTW201-liters, met elke syfer terug na sy faktuur en foto.

FarmOps

Power, water, growing and stock, run from one screen.Krag, water, kweek en voorraad, vanuit een skerm bestuur.

You see your whole farm on one screen: solar and battery, irrigation, grow rooms and stock, with the standby generator coming. Every power outage is counted and costed, every field has a rain book and a water budget, and every bag of feed or fertiliser is booked to where it went. Describe a rule in plain words — “start the pump when the battery is above 80%” — and FarmOps writes the automation for you to approve.

Books, VAT and wages, done the way a farm works.Boeke, BTW en lone, soos 'n plaas werk.

On Friday the wages work out daily rates, UIF, advances, farm store and rations, then print the payslips, the cash-envelope breakdown and the EFT list, so you pay in minutes and SARS and Labour get figures that add up.

Jou boeke hou hulself.
