“Is it running hot?”“Loop hy warm?”

“Is it still charging?”“Laai hy nog?”

The volt reading shows a failing charging system before it leaves you on the side of the road.Die volt-lesing wys 'n laaistelsel wat ingee voor dit jou langs die pad los.

“The fuel gauge lies.”“Die petrolmeter jok.”

A real tank reading, so you stop guessing how far the next town is.'n Regte tenklesing, so jy hou op raai hoe ver die volgende dorp is.
